# Study Room and Co-Working Space Booking System

COE420 Team 2 (Fall 2026): Sobia Adnan, Salama AlSuwaidi, and Mais Abudaqqa.

A proposed **web-based platform open to anyone**—students, freelancers, professionals, remote workers, and other individuals—to search for and book study rooms, meeting rooms, private rooms, desks, and co-working spaces. The platform is **not campus-only**.

Users can filter by location, date/time, capacity, and facilities; view details and availability; and create, modify, view, or cancel bookings. Platform administrators manage users, listings, reservations, availability, and reports. **Third-party space providers** are external organizations that supply space details and availability through an **external API**, not platform administrators. External data must be validated and availability checked before confirming a booking.
