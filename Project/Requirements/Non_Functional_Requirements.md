# Non-Functional Requirements

## Sobia Adnan – g00100257

**NFR-01 – Performance:** The system shall display space search results within 3 seconds under normal operating conditions.

**NFR-02 – Security:** The system shall require users to log in before making or cancelling a booking.

**NFR-03 – Usability:** The system shall display space details, including facilities and location, in a clear and understandable format.

**NFR-04 – Reliability:** The system shall update space availability after a booking is made or cancelled, ensuring that the displayed availability reflects the latest booking status.

**NFR-05 – Portability:** The web-based system shall support access through Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Mozilla Firefox.

## Salama Alsuwaidi – g00099875

**NFR-06 – Maintainability:** The system shall let administrators update room details and booking rules from the dashboard without editing the code.

**NFR-07 – Scalability:** The system shall support 500 simultaneous users and finish search or booking actions within few seconds.

**NFR-08 – Responsiveness:** The system shall adjust its layout to fit desktop, tablet, and mobile screens without horizontal scrolling.

**NFR-09 – Robustness:** The system shall show an error message when users enter invalid data or try to book an unavailable room.

**NFR-10 – Accessibility:** The system shall allow users to search for rooms and manage their bookings using only a keyboard.

## Mais Abudaqqa – g00099729

**NFR-11 – Availability:** The system shall be available at least 99% of the time during normal operating hours, excluding planned maintenance.

**NFR-12 – Security:** The system shall lock a user account for 5 minutes after 5 consecutive unsuccessful login attempts.

**NFR-13 – Recoverability:** The system shall create a backup of user and booking data at least once every 24 hours so that the data can be restored in case of system failure.

**NFR-14 – Privacy:** The system shall restrict access to users' personal information and booking history to the user and authorized administrators.

**NFR-15 – Data Integrity:** The system shall ensure that two users cannot successfully book the same space for overlapping date and time periods.
