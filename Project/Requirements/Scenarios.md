# Scenarios

## S-01 – Search and Book a Study Room
**Actor/Stakeholder:** Student

A student needs a quiet room for group study. The student searches for study rooms based on location, date, time, duration, and capacity. The student filters the available spaces based on facilities such as Wi-Fi and whiteboards. After viewing the space details and availability, the student selects a suitable room and makes a booking.

## S-02 – Cancel an Existing Booking
**Actor/Stakeholder:** Student

A student has an existing booking for a study room but no longer needs it. The student views their upcoming bookings, selects the reservation, and cancels it. The system updates the availability of the space after the booking is cancelled.

## S-03 – Modify an Existing Booking
**Actor/Stakeholder:** Student

A student has already booked a study room but needs to change their study session. They log into the system and open their upcoming bookings. After choosing the booking they want to adjust, they select a new date, time, or room. The system checks whether the new option is available and updates the booking if it is. The student then gets a confirmation message. If the new time isn't available, the system shows other possible choices.

## S-04 – Manage Workspace Details
**Actor/Stakeholder:** Space Administrator

A space administrator wants to add a new study room or update an existing one. They open the admin dashboard and enter details such as the room name, capacity, facilities, and available times. The administrator saves the changes, and the updated room information becomes available in the system. Users can then see the correct details when searching for a space.

## S-05 – Receive a Booking Reminder
**Actor/Stakeholder:** Student

A student has an upcoming booking for a study room. Before the scheduled time, the system sends the student a reminder with the booking details, including the room, date, start time, and duration. The student can open their booking to check the details and make sure everything is correct.
