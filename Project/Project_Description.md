**short project description:**

Existing workspace booking systems in the UAE are mainly provided by educational institutions or commercial co-working spaces. These systems are often limited to specific institutions or focus mainly on professional users, so individuals who need a flexible place to study, work, meet, or collaborate do not have one place to search for and reserve a suitable space.

The Study Room and Co-Working Space Booking System addresses this gap through a unified web-based platform for students, professionals, freelancers, remote workers, and other individuals. Users can search for meeting rooms and desks (day passes) by location, date, time, duration, capacity, and available facilities, and the system filters the results according to their needs.

The platform works with third-party space providers through the DeskOS API. Coworking operators that join DeskOS choose which of their campuses to share, and the platform reads their cities, campuses, spaces, and availability through this single API. It also creates and cancels bookings through it. The system displays the operator's name, operator ID, campus, and city alongside each space. DeskOS does not allow a booking to be changed, so a change is made by cancelling the booking and booking again.

**The main objectives of the project are to:**

1.  Provide one web-based platform where students, professionals,
    freelancers, remote workers, and other individuals can find and
    reserve suitable spaces for studying, working, meetings, or
    collaboration.

2.  Allow users to search for spaces based on location, date, time,
    duration, capacity, and available facilities.

3.  Allow users to make, view, change, and cancel bookings, and to
    receive booking reminders and availability notifications.

4.  Get spaces and their availability from DeskOS operators through the
    DeskOS API, so users can search external spaces in one place.

5.  Allow the website administrator to manage platform users, access
    permissions, platform activity, authorized booking records,
    reported issues, and usage reports.

The platform manages its own user accounts, access permissions, booking records, and notifications. It does not create or edit the operators' space listings or availability, and the administrator does not manage the physical facilities, source listings, or source availability of external providers.

Reference: DeskOS, "Aggregator Partner API," DeskOS Help Center. https://help.deskos.net/docs/api/aggregators
