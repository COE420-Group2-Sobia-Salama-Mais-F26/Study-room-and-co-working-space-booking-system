# Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest/Expectations | Influence on the System | How the System Affects the Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outside (Students) | Use the system to search for and book study rooms and co-working spaces. | Easy booking, suitable spaces, accurate availability, and convenient facilities. | Their needs and feedback help determine the system's features and design. | They can find and reserve suitable study spaces more easily and manage their bookings online. |
| Space administrators | Manage users, spaces, availability, and reservations. | Easy management, accurate information, system reliability, and useful reports. | They control system data and help ensure that the system operates correctly. | They can manage the platform more efficiently and monitor bookings and space usage. |
| Stakeholders inside (Government / Regulatory Authorities) | Ensure that the system and participating businesses follow relevant UAE rules and regulations. | Compliance with applicable regulations, user safety, and proper handling of information. | Regulations may affect how user information, bookings, and businesses are managed. | The system can support more organized and regulated workspace services. |
| Existing UAE Workspace Platforms | Existing companies that provide workspace booking services in the UAE. | They may be interested in collaborating, expanding their customer reach, or investing in and supporting the proposed platform. | Their existing services and experience can help identify market needs and useful features. They could also provide feedback or partnerships. | They could collaborate with the platform by listing their spaces, reaching more users, or potentially investing in the system and benefiting from additional bookings. |
