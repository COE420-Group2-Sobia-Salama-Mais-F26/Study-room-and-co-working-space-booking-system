# Project Risk Register

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Possible Cause | Probability | Impact | Mitigation/Response Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Requirements may change during the project. | The team may realize that some features are missing or the customer may request changes. | Medium | Medium | Discuss the requirements with the team early and agree on the main features before starting development. |
| R2 | The project may not be completed on time. | Some tasks may take longer than expected or team members may fall behind. | Medium | High | Divide the project into smaller tasks, set deadlines, and regularly check the progress of each team member. |
| R3 | Communication problems may occur between team members. | Team members may misunderstand tasks or fail to update each other about their progress. | Medium | Medium | Have regular team meetings and use a shared platform to keep track of tasks and important updates. |
| R4 | User or booking data could be exposed or lost. | Weak passwords, poor database security, or mistakes in handling user information. | Low | High | Use secure login methods, protect the database, validate user input, and make regular backups. |
| R5 | The team may have technical difficulties while developing the system. | Some team members may not have enough experience with the programming language, database, or other tools. | Medium | High | Research the problem, use tutorials and documentation, and help each other when someone is stuck. |
